## Goal

Stereo and surround sources played on two speakers with sound placed beyond and around them, for one listener.
The app cancels the sound each speaker sends to the opposite ear (crosstalk cancellation). The canceller is
designed from the speaker angle or from the listener's own in-ear measurement, and follows the head by camera.
This is the BACCH4Mac class of product, inside the same app as room correction.

## Design note

Outline. The full note (derivation, latency, stage order, rate and channel assumptions, reset behaviour) is the first
item of S1, as AGENTS.md sections 4 and 10 require.

- **Maths (changed 17 Nov 2026, decision 3).** The two-by-two matrix of speaker-to-ear responses is inverted with one
  constant regularisation value (Kirkeby, Nelson, Hamada and Orduna-Bustamante, "Fast deconvolution of multichannel
  systems using regularization", IEEE Transactions on Speech and Audio Processing, 1998), or with a fixed curve,
  chosen in advance, that only limits the working band (Kirkeby and Nelson, "Digital filter design for inversion
  problems in sound reproduction", Journal of the Audio Engineering Society, 1999). Both citations are from memory;
  S1 checks them. Headroom comes from one overall gain reduction through E9 and from the strength control.
- **What the design must not do.** US 9,167,344 (Princeton, the BACCH filter; shown as active, adjusted expiry
  18 Apr 2032) claims a regularisation value that varies with frequency, calculated from the inverted matrix, so that
  the filters are flat at the speaker inputs over a band or part of one. So this design has no regularisation
  calculated per frequency from the inverse and no clamping of the filter gain to a flat level; the earlier "boost
  cap" is dropped. Our own code does not avoid a patent; only a different method does. No code, filter data or tuning
  from any product is used (AGENTS.md section 1). The claims were read on Google Patents on 17 Nov 2026; this is not
  legal advice (G1).
- **Cost, accepted (decision 3).** More tonal colour than BACCH at the same strength, or less separation at the same
  colour. E22 measures how audible it is.
- **Two sources for the responses.** Model mode (changed 17 Nov 2026, decision 4): the speaker angle and distance from
  E29 (or typed in) as two point sources in free space, with no head model. US 11,589,162 (Google, from Dysonics;
  shown as active, anticipated expiry Nov 2039) claims crosstalk filter sets designed with pressure matching and a
  spherical head model, so the E13 head model is not used here. Measured mode: the E13 in-ear capture at the seat, at
  the centre and, for head follow, to the left and right. It uses the listener's own measured responses, not a model.
- **Engine.** Design maths runs off the realtime thread in `core/` (C++), deterministic. The realtime part is a
  two-in two-out FIR on the front pair, built off-thread and swapped atomically. The old one-knob model is replaced,
  not kept beside it. Stored values of the old model are ignored (it was never reachable from the production UI).
- **Surround from two speakers.** On a two-speaker system, 5.1 and 7.1 sources may go through the E13
  virtual-speaker renderer and then the canceller, as a choice beside the E25 fold-down.
- **Head follow.** The E4 S26b head position selects and blends filter sets. Camera rules are E4 S26b's: the camera
  light is on only while head follow is on, and it stops on disable, sleep and background. With head follow off
  the sweet spot is fixed.
- **Safety.** No microphone is in the loop while playing, so this is not live ANC or MIMO treatment and has no arm.
  Headroom is taken through E9 as one overall gain reduction, and protection sees the boosted signal.
- **Rates.** Every standard rate from 44.1 to 384 kHz.
- **Limits the page states.** One listener. Best with the speakers close and few early reflections. Weaker in a
  lively room. Some tonal change is expected. The app never uses the BACCH name (a Princeton trademark) and never
  claims an uncoloured result.
- Only Swift reads the flag. No slice makes `AudioIOEngine.swift` longer.

## Owner decisions (17 Nov 2026, whole-app review)

1. **E35 is in v1**, not the v2 roadmap.
2. **Surround from two speakers** for 5.1 and 7.1 sources is included.
3. **Filter method** (17 Nov 2026, after reading the patents): constant or fixed-curve regularisation only. No
   regularisation calculated per frequency from the inverse and no clamping to a flat level. The cost in tonal
   colour or separation against BACCH is accepted.
4. **Model mode** uses free-space geometry only, with no head model.
5. **A patent attorney reviews the written design note** before any S1 code is added. The owner records the answer
   in G1 and G4.
