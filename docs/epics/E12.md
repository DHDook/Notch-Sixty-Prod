## Goal

A new user picks an output, says what speakers they have, and is walked through levels and
polarity, distances, a multi-seat sweep, bass, correction and a post-apply verification to a saved,
verified profile, in one flow and without opening any expert workspace. Speakers, Speaker
Calibration, Measurement Lab and Room Correction become one System area, with the room geometry
and treatment advisor inside it and inside the flow. The flow wraps E10's correction and bass and
E11's measurement suite; it adds no new DSP. The software is done when the flow reaches a verified
calibration end to end against the synthetic reference room at all eight rates. The usability test
with a first-time user runs with hardware in E22.

## Design note

1. **System area shape.** The sidebar's System group becomes one **System** row plus
   **Headphones** and **Active Acoustics** (unchanged). System has a tab bar: **Overview** (status
   of the selected output's calibration and the "Set up my system" entry), **Speakers** (today's
   Speakers view), **Room** (geometry and treatment advisor, moved from Tools), **Measure**
   (Measurement Lab) and **Calibrate** (Speaker Calibration and Room Correction as two sections of
   one page: alignment, then bass and correction, then verification). Each existing workspace is
   reused as the tab's content in S1; deeper merging of duplicated cards (microphone, seats) happens
   in S7. The old `ProductionSection` cases stay as aliases that map to `(.system, tab)` so menu
   commands and stored selections keep working.
2. **Flow model.** `SystemSetupFlow` is a pure Swift value type (no SwiftUI, no engine) with an
   ordered step list and a reducer: `start`, `next`, `back`, `skip`, `fail`, `cancel`, `finish`.
   Steps: Output → Topology → Microphone and level → Room (optional) → Measure seats → Alignment
   (levels, polarity, distances) → Bass → Correction → Verify → Save. Each step declares its
   preconditions and what it produces; `next` is refused with a reason until they hold. Steps are
   driven through a `SystemSetupServices` protocol (measure, design, apply, revert, verify, save),
   implemented by the real controllers and by a simulated service over the reference room for tests.
3. **One measurement pass.** The flow measures once: each physical source at each seat, through the
   existing campaign transport (`MultichannelCalibrationController`), multi-mic when several mics
   are present (E11 S9b), otherwise one seat at a time with the seat picker. The seat records feed
   both the calibration designer (alignment) and a `RoomCorrectionProject` (bass and correction)
   through one adapter, so the user never re-measures for correction. Expert workspaces keep their
   own flows.
4. **Topology without E8.** The Topology step offers 2.0, 2.1, 2.2 and the layouts the current
   routing supports, with crossover frequency and slope from today's `BassManagementConfiguration`.
   It writes only through the existing profile APIs. When E8 lands, E8 replaces the step's
   crossover controls with its own editor; the step's contract (a routing and a bass-management
   configuration) stays.
5. **Safe levels.** The level step plays band-limited pink noise from the E11 generator starting at
   −40 dBFS, ramps only on user request, stops at the existing generator ceiling, and reads the SPL
   meter to set a sweep level near 75 dB(C) at the main seat. The flow never enables live ANC or
   MIMO treatment and never touches Active Acoustics.
6. **Apply, revert, cancel.** At start the flow snapshots the selected `PlaybackSystemState`.
   Alignment, bass and correction are applied as trials; Cancel at any step restores the snapshot
   exactly. Measurement steps require playback stopped (today's rule) and the flow stops and
   restarts playback itself with a visible explanation; verification runs through the live graph.
7. **Verification.** The Verify step always re-measures the main seat after apply and shows
   predicted against measured. For mixed-phase and joint-bass designs it is E10 S8's verifier and
   gates `canDeploy`; failure reverts and returns to Correction with the reason. For minimum-phase
   it is informational. A profile saved without a passing report is labelled "Not verified".
8. **Room in the flow.** Room is an optional step before measuring: dimensions and placement from
   `RoomGeometryPlacement`, with seat and speaker suggestions that pre-fill seat names. After
   verification, a "Room insights" card shows the advisor's findings for problems EQ cannot fix
   (decay from E11 RT60, modal nulls left after correction) and links to the Room tab.
9. **Persistence and resume.** A `SystemSetupSession` (schema 1, JSON, one per playback system in
   Application Support) stores the step, choices and measurement IDs, never audio buffers. Quitting
   resumes at the last completed step; a change of output device or sample rate invalidates the
   measurement steps and says why. Finishing saves the profile through `saveCurrentSystemProfile`
   with the calibration summary and verification report.
10. **Entry points.** A "Set up my system" card on the Dashboard and System Overview when the
    selected output has no verified calibration, and a menu item. No automatic sheet at first launch
    (open question 3). Headphone outputs show a pointer to Headphones instead.
11. **Rates.** The flow is rate-agnostic; it records the rate it measured at and relies on E10's
    per-rate synthesis for other rates. Its end-to-end test runs at all eight standard rates.

## Feature flag

`e12.guided-setup` ("Guided setup and System area", epic E12) was added to `FeatureFlag.all` in S1
and removed in S7 (#243). It had gated the System area, the flow and its entry points. Both
are now always on: the System area, the flow and the entry cards ship without a flag, and the old
sidebar rows are gone (old sections remain as aliases to System tabs).
