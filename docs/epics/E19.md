# E19 MIMO room treatment qualification, ART class

## Status

## Goal

Active low-frequency decay control (Dirac ART class) that uses two or more subwoofers and the mains
to shorten modal decay below 150 Hz at several seats, on the unified graph at every rate from 44.1
to 384 kHz. The user picks which speakers take part and over what range, sets an explicit bass time
target, sees the predicted T20/T30 before and after at each seat, and can arm live treatment only
through the PR75 verification path and the E4 arming conditions.

## Design note

1. **Core first.** Design maths move to C++ in `core/include/n60/mimo.h` and `core/src/mimo/`
   (C ABI, own `N60_MIMO_ABI_VERSION`, Linux CI). Swift keeps thin wrappers. The legacy C headers
   and the bridge runtime stay frozen for the legacy path until E23 deletes them (S6 adds the
   matching E23 item).
2. **Plant.** For source s and seat m, the plant is the raw per-output IR captured by the E24
   measurement flow, convolved with the lane's known linear chain downstream of the MIMO stage
   (ROOM_FIR, SPEAKER_IR, DELAY, per-output OUTPUT_GAIN trim, polarity) from the published
   snapshot. No re-measurement after room correction. S2 confirms E24 stores raw per-output IRs
   with correction bypassed; if not, it records a scoped gap.
3. **Target (explicit time and decay).** For seat m the target IR is the reference path (the mains
   pair summed, or the LFE path for LFE content) shifted to the **bass time target** τ and multiplied
   after its direct arrival by exp(-6.91 t / T_target), so the target decays with T60 = T_target.
   τ is the arrival of treated bass at the primary seat relative to the mains' direct arrival
   (Q1); it is also the modelling delay that makes the inverse causal. Solver: per-bin Tikhonov
   least squares (Kirkeby and Nelson, 1999) on a dense grid (bin spacing at most 1/filter length)
   from 15 to 200 Hz, frequency-dependent regularisation, then IFFT, window and causality check.
   Delayed-source decay cancellation follows Celestinos and Nielsen (CABS, 2008).
4. **Support range and exclusion.** Each source has a support range [low, high] Hz (defaults: mains
   from the measured -10 dB point to 150 Hz; subs from their -10 dB point to crossover + one
   octave). Outside it the regularisation weight rises 24 dB/octave, so effort there is at least
   40 dB down. An excluded source plays its own program only: its row of the treatment delta is
   exactly zero. A source excluded or without measurements at an included seat is refused by name.
5. **Multirate stage.** New `N60_STAGE_MIMO` (ABI v32), position 13.5: after BASS_MGMT and
   STEREO_CROSSOVER, before ROOM_FIR, so the hard limit (E9 S5) and PROTECTION cover treatment.
   Delta form: y_s = x_s z^-L + up(Σ_t E_st * down(x_t)), E = C - I z^-τ, so above the treatment band
   the program is untouched and arming changes only the delta mix. The low band runs at fs/D with
   D = 16, 32, 64, 128 for the 44.1/48, 88.2/96, 176.4/192, 352.8/384 families (2.756 or 3 kHz),
   linear-phase halfband cascade, up to 4,096 low-rate taps (1.37 s), up to 8 sources (Q4).
   Latency L = resampler delay + τ, the same for every lane, armed or not; it is declared and
   latency-matched. The design is computed at fs/D, so one design serves a rate family.
6. **Safety per contributing speaker.**
   - Headroom: the stage is added as a term to E9 S6's `n60/headroom.h` (no second headroom
     model). The design is refused, or re-solved with more regularisation (at most 4 steps), until
     each source's predicted peak gain is at most its headroom budget (3 dB reserved by default,
     the existing declaration value, now computed rather than declared).
   - Excursion: per-source effort cap below the support range, falling 12 dB/octave below the
     source's lower limit (the `ExcursionLimits` rule applied per source, measured only).
   - Thermal: no driver data, so a long-term budget: treatment delta RMS in band at most -6 dB
     re program RMS, integrated over 10 s (Q5); the bench thermal soak is E22.
   - Runtime: the stage meters the delta per source against the design's predicted bound; 6 dB over
     for 50 ms latches a fault, fades the delta out in 10 ms and disarms.
7. **Authorisation (PR75 path, criteria unchanged).** Permit = software verification (S7: predicted
   decay and spatial criteria from the existing verifier) + treated repeat measurement + hardware
   acceptance record, bound to a fingerprint (taps hash, sources, support ranges, τ, output UID,
   rate family, room-correction and downstream-chain fingerprint, layout). The gate's band comes
   from the design, not 20/150 Hz. Whether hardware acceptance is per installation or a product
   qualification done once in E22 is Q3; until the owner decides, the criteria stay as written and
   no permit can be issued in production.
8. **Arming (E4 owner conditions of 2 Nov 2026).** `LiveTreatmentArm(.mimoRoomTreatment)`: user
   initiated only; never on launch, wake, device or rate change; 3 s fade-in that starts only
   after the gates pass (valid permit and fingerprint, stage attached, hard limit present, engine
   lease); 10 ms trip; never persisted; any trip or teardown returns it to off. Master volume is
   common to all lanes and does not disarm; a per-output trim or downstream-chain change breaks
   the fingerprint and disarms. A graph rebuild never fades through live treatment: the delta is
   muted in both graphs and re-arms only through the arm path (E6 safety exception).
9. **Scope edges.** Routing matrix (E8) stays refused (Q6). Two-channel DACs keep E25 S6's L/R-only
   sources. E10's joint bass stays static and is the input to E19, not replaced by it. E26 S8
   (post-v1) still needs the owner's written MIMO authorisation. Only Swift reads the flag.

`n60_measure_predict_treated_ir` (sum over sources of plant * filter, at the device rate or fs/D)
and a reference-room extension: per-source modal gain and phase so two subs can cancel a mode.
- [ ] Identity filters give exactly the measured sum; a known filter matches an offline convolution

- [ ] Third-octave T30 from 25 to 160 Hz matches the room's analytic mode RT60 within 5 percent.
- [ ] Linux CI; ASan clean. Bench: none.

### S2 `e19/mimo-design-core` (needs-owner: MIMO design maths), about 1,300 lines

- [ ] Reference room, 2 mains + 2 subs, 3 seats, modes at 40 and 70 Hz with 0.8 s RT60: predicted

- [ ] Excluded source: delta row exactly zero. Effort outside a support range at least 40 dB down.
- [ ] Treated arrival at the primary seat within one low-rate sample of τ.

## Bench checks (all deferred to E22)

1. Two or more subs plus mains: third-octave T20/T30 from 25 to 160 Hz before and after at three or
   more seats; level shift at each seat; REW cross-check of the same captures.
2. Treated repeat measurement through the S7 path; spatial variance before and after.
3. Excursion: listen for driver distress at reference level plus 6 dB with treatment armed.
4. Thermal soak: 1 h band-limited pink noise at reference level, treatment armed.
5. Lifecycle with treatment armed: Stop/Start, unplug/replug of one sub (fault fade), sleep/wake,
   rate change, normal Quit and force-quit; the 3 s fade-in and 10 ms trip are click-free.
6. Loopback peak per output with treatment armed against the hard limit.
7. CPU at 384 kHz with 8 sources on the slowest supported Mac.
8. The PR75 hardware acceptance fields (thermal, protection chain, stop/start, unplug/replug,
   sleep/wake, audible artefact).

## Open questions for the owner

1. **Bass time target and latency budget.** Recommend τ default 0 ms re the mains' arrival, range up
   to 20 ms, total added latency at most 25 ms, and MIMO refused in the Video latency mode.
2. **Decay target default.** Recommend the room's 500 Hz–1 kHz T30 from E11, clamped to 0.15–0.4 s.
3. **PR75 hardware acceptance.** Today it must be complete per installation, so no customer could
   ever arm. Recommend: keep the criteria as written now; after E22, turn the hardware fields into
   a product qualification per configuration class (source count, rate family) done once on the
   bench, leaving users the software verification, treated repeat measurement and arm.
4. **Source count.** Recommend raising 4 to 8 contributing sources.
5. **Thermal without driver data.** Recommend the long-term budget in design note 6 (delta RMS at
   most -6 dB re program over 10 s), checked on the bench in E22.
6. **Routing matrix.** Recommend keeping treatment refused with E8 routing in v1.
