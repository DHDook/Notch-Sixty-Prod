## Goal

Active bass decay control (Dirac ART class): two or more subs plus the mains shorten modal decay
below 150 Hz at several seats, on the unified graph at every rate to 384 kHz, with or without E8
routing (bi/tri-amp speakers included). The user picks the speakers and their ranges, sets an
explicit bass time target, sees predicted T20/T30 per seat, and arms only through the PR75 path and
the E4 arming conditions.

## Design note

1. **Core first.** Design maths move to C++ in `core/include/n60/mimo.h` and `core/src/mimo/`
   (C ABI, own `N60_MIMO_ABI_VERSION`, Linux CI). Swift keeps thin wrappers. The legacy C headers
   and the bridge runtime stay frozen for the legacy path until E23 deletes them (S6 adds the
   matching E23 item).
2. **Plant.** For source s and seat m, the plant is the raw per-output IR captured by the E24
   measurement flow, convolved with the lane's known linear chain downstream of the MIMO stage
   (unrouted: ROOM_FIR, SPEAKER_IR, DELAY, per-output OUTPUT_GAIN trim, polarity; routed: note 10)
   from the published snapshot. No re-measurement after room correction. S2 confirms E24 stores raw
   per-output IRs with correction bypassed; if not, it records a scoped gap.
3. **Target (explicit time and decay).** For seat m the target IR is the reference path (the mains
   summed, or the LFE path for LFE content) shifted to the **bass time target** τ and multiplied
   after its direct arrival by exp(-6.91 t / T_target), so the target decays with T60 = T_target.
   τ is the arrival of treated bass at the primary seat relative to the mains' direct arrival
   (D1); it is also the modelling delay that makes the inverse causal. T_target defaults per D2.
   Solver: per-bin Tikhonov least squares (Kirkeby and Nelson, 1999) on a dense grid (bin spacing
   at most 1/filter length) from 15 to 200 Hz, frequency-dependent regularisation, then IFFT, window
   and causality check. Delayed-source decay cancellation follows Celestinos and Nielsen (CABS, 2008).
4. **Support range and exclusion.** Each source has a support range [low, high] Hz (defaults: mains
   from the measured -10 dB point to 150 Hz; subs from their -10 dB point to crossover + one
   octave). Outside it the regularisation weight rises 24 dB/octave, so effort there is at least
   40 dB down. An excluded source plays its own program only: its row of the treatment delta is
   exactly zero. A source excluded or without measurements at an included seat is refused by name.
5. **Multirate stage.** New `N60_STAGE_MIMO` (ABI v32), position 13.5: after BASS_MGMT and
   STEREO_CROSSOVER, before ROOM_FIR, so the hard limit (E9 S5) and PROTECTION cover treatment.
   Delta form: y_s = x_s z^-L + up(Σ_t E_st * down(x_t)), E = C - I z^-τ, so above the treatment band
   the program is untouched and arming changes only the delta mix. The low band runs at fs/D with
   D = 16, 32, 64, 128 for the 44.1/48, 88.2/96, 176.4/192, 352.8/384 families (2.756 or 3 kHz),
   linear-phase halfband cascade, up to 4,096 low-rate taps (1.37 s), up to 8 sources (D4).
   Latency L = resampler delay + τ, the same for every lane, armed or not, at most 25 ms (D1); it is
   declared and latency-matched. One design serves a rate family. E9's state-carry table lists both
   MIMO kinds as warm-in-full (no lockstep carry); the delta is muted across a swap anyway (note 8).
6. **Safety per contributing speaker.**
   - Headroom: the stage is added as a term to E9 S6's `n60/headroom.h` (no second headroom
     model). The design is refused, or re-solved with more regularisation (at most 4 steps), until
     each source's predicted peak gain is at most its headroom budget (3 dB reserved by default,
     the existing declaration value, now computed rather than declared).
   - Excursion: per-source effort cap below the support range, falling 12 dB/octave below the
     source's lower limit (the `ExcursionLimits` rule applied per source, measured only).
   - Thermal (D5): treatment delta RMS in band at most -6 dB re program RMS, integrated over 10 s;
     the bench thermal soak is E22.
   - Runtime: the stage meters the delta per source against the design's predicted bound; 6 dB over
     for 50 ms latches a fault, fades the delta out in 10 ms and disarms.
7. **Authorisation (PR75 path, criteria unchanged, D3).** Permit = software verification (S7:
   predicted decay and spatial criteria from the existing verifier) + treated repeat measurement +
   hardware acceptance record, bound to a fingerprint (taps hash, sources, support ranges, τ, output
   UID, rate family, room-correction and downstream-chain fingerprint, layout, routed fields of note
   10). The gate's band comes from the design, not 20/150 Hz. Until E22 fills a product qualification
   for the configuration class, no permit can be issued in production.
8. **Arming (E4 owner conditions of 2 Nov 2026).** `LiveTreatmentArm(.mimoRoomTreatment)`: user
   initiated only; never on launch, wake, device or rate change; 3 s fade-in that starts only
   after the gates pass (valid permit and fingerprint, stage attached, hard limit present, engine
   lease); 10 ms trip; never persisted; any trip or teardown returns it to off. Master volume is
   common to all lanes and does not disarm; a per-output trim or downstream-chain change breaks
   the fingerprint and disarms. A graph rebuild never fades through live treatment: the delta is
   muted in both graphs and re-arms only through the arm path (E6 safety exception).
9. **Scope edges.** Two-channel DACs keep E25 S6's L/R-only sources. E10's joint bass stays static
   and is the input to E19, not replaced by it. E26 S8 (post-v1) still needs the owner's written
   MIMO authorisation. Only Swift reads the flag.
10. **Routed outputs (E8 routing; owner decision of 10 Nov 2026).**
    - Source = physical speaker. Each `RoutedSpeaker` is one MIMO source, whatever its way count.
      Outputs fed only by raw matrix entries are not sources (delta exactly zero) and are shown as
      "add as a speaker to include".
    - Placement. New `N60_STAGE_MIMO_ROUTED` (next ABI after v32), position 23.55: after ROUTE,
      before XOVER, DRIVER_FIR, DELAY and PROTECTION. It shares S4's kernel and parameters and adds
      an injection table. ROOM_FIR, SPEAKER_IR and OUTPUT_GAIN are upstream in routed graphs, so the
      inputs already carry room correction and trims.
    - Input and injection. Speaker s's input is its lowest in-band way lane on entry (its
      pre-crossover feed). Its delta is added, before XOVER, to every **in-band way**: a way whose
      passband (high-pass corner, or measured -10 dB point when it has none, to its low-pass
      corner) overlaps the support range (Q7). Other ways get exactly zero delta. At most 4 in-band
      ways per speaker; more is refused by name. Each injected delta then passes through that way's
      own crossover, EQ, linearisation FIR, trim, polarity, delay and limiter like the program.
    - Plants. Program plant P_sm = Σ over all ways (raw way IR at seat m ∘ way chain from
      `n60/xover.h`, DRIVER_FIR, DELAY). Injection plant Q_sm = the same sum over in-band ways. The
      solver uses P for the reference path and the uncontrolled response and Q for the control
      path; unrouted, P = Q. A way whose high-pass is at least 400 Hz is modelled from its predicted
      chain with no capture, provided it is at least 40 dB down below 200 Hz. In-band ways need a
      raw IR at every included seat (Q9); missing captures are refused by name.
    - Support range from ways: default low = max(lowest in-band way's high-pass corner, measured
      -10 dB point); default high = min(150 Hz, highest in-band way's low-pass corner).
    - Headroom per output: E9 S6 evaluates every way lane; E19 adds the MIMO_ROUTED term per
      injection lane (delta through that way's chain). Budget per lane = min(hard-limit headroom,
      the way limiter's threshold headroom) less 3 dB, so the way limiter never acts on a valid
      design. Who adds the ROUTE, XOVER and DRIVER_FIR terms to `headroom.h` is Q8.
    - Excursion per way: effort into each in-band way falls 12 dB/octave below max(way high-pass
      corner, measured -10 dB point). The thermal budget (D5) applies per speaker.
    - Fingerprint adds: speaker-to-way map, every ROUTE entry (topology and gain), every way's XOVER
      parameters, DRIVER_FIR taps hash, way delay, trim, polarity, limiter and endpoint. Any live
      edit of these (ROUTE gain, XOVER corner, trim, polarity) disarms with the 10 ms fade.
    - Configuration class (D3) adds routed or unrouted and the in-band way count per speaker.
    - Dependencies: E8 S11 (#431, stereo crossover into sub lanes) and S11b (N-channel crossover
      wiring) settle which lanes exist before ROUTE; E9 S6 supplies the headroom model.

## Owner decisions (10 Nov 2026)

- **D1 (Q1) Bass time target and latency.** τ default 0 ms re the mains' arrival, range 0–20 ms;
  total added latency at most 25 ms; MIMO refused in the Video latency mode.
- **D2 (Q2) Decay target default.** The room's 500 Hz–1 kHz T30 from E11, clamped to 0.15–0.4 s.
- **D3 (Q3) PR75 hardware acceptance.** Criteria stay as written now. After E22, the hardware fields
  become a product qualification per configuration class (source count, rate family, routed or
  not) done once on the bench; users keep software verification, treated repeat measurement and arm.
- **D4 (Q4) Source count.** Raised from 4 to 8 contributing sources.
- **D5 (Q5) Thermal without driver data.** Long-term budget: delta RMS at most -6 dB re program
  over 10 s; checked on the bench in E22.
- **D6 (Q6, changed) Routing.** Treatment runs with E8 routing; design note 10, slices S9–S10.

## Bench checks (all deferred to E22)

1. Two or more subs plus mains: third-octave T20/T30 from 25 to 160 Hz before and after at three or
   more seats; level shift at each seat; REW cross-check of the same captures.
2. Treated repeat measurement through the S7 path; spatial variance before and after.
3. Excursion: listen for driver distress at reference level plus 6 dB with treatment armed.
4. Thermal soak: 1 h band-limited pink noise at reference level, treatment armed.
5. Lifecycle with treatment armed: Stop/Start, unplug/replug of one sub (fault fade), sleep/wake,
   rate change, normal Quit and force-quit; the 3 s fade-in and 10 ms trip are click-free.
6. Loopback peak per output with treatment armed against the hard limit.
7. CPU at 384 kHz with 8 sources on the slowest supported Mac.
8. The PR75 hardware acceptance fields (thermal, protection chain, stop/start, unplug/replug,
   sleep/wake, audible artefact), recorded as the D3 class qualification.
9. Routed: bi-amp and tri-amp mains plus two subs, checks 1, 3 and 6 per way lane; tweeter and mid
   outputs show no treatment energy; way limiters never act at reference level plus 6 dB.
10. Routed lifecycle: a live crossover or trim edit while armed disarms click-free.

- **D7 (Q7) Which ways get the delta.** Automatic: every way whose passband overlaps the support
  range. No per-way override in v1.
- **D8 (Q8) Routed headroom terms.** E9 S6 (or an E9 follow-up) adds ROUTE, XOVER and DRIVER_FIR
  terms to `headroom.h`; E19 S9 adds only its MIMO term.
- **D9 (Q9) Per-way captures.** Require a raw IR per in-band way at every included seat; ways with a
  high-pass at 400 Hz or more are modelled. S9 records a gap if the measurement flow cannot capture them.

## Post-E22 ideas (not scheduled)

- **Per-way delta override.** Let the owner include or exclude individual ways from the delta (for
  example exclude a small mid-woofer with low excursion headroom, or include a way the automatic rule
  skips). Needs a per-way flag in the routing/speaker profile, a way mask in the arm fingerprint and
  C ABI (additive field), refusal rules (no seat left without a controllable way), a UI toggle, and
  per-class tests plus a bench check. About 400–600 lines. Revisit once E22 bench data shows where
  the automatic rule is wrong.
