## Goal

Fix the defects found in the 15 Nov 2026 review of the Equalizer, Dynamics and Meters pages:
wrong behaviour, hidden errors, audible glitches and wasted CPU. Defects only; feature gaps and
UX redesign are a later epic. Review items are cited as R1–R22 (R14 is a note for the owner, not

## Design note

1. **Engine code home.** Every engine fix lands in the C++ core. `N60Protection.c`, `N60Dynamics.c` and
   `N60RenderKernel.c` stay frozen (E27 decision 6; E23 deletes them). R11 and R13 close if their
   confirmation shows legacy-only reach. If R7 or R12 is found on a default production path through
   `N60Protection.c` (binaural headphone bridge without a graph), record a `scoped` gap for E23; do not
   edit the C.
2. **One EQ band rule.** New `NotchSixty/Audio/EQBandRules.swift` (pure Swift, no engine state) holds the
   limits (frequency 20 Hz to 192 kHz, Q from the page's range, the gain range, dynamic ranges, notch
   cut-only) and `renderState(band, sampleRate) -> .active | .aboveNyquist | .invalid(reason)`. The page,
   `sanitize`, the parser, `StereoPlaybackControl` and both `AudioIOEngine` validators call it; the
   duplicated rules are deleted, so `AudioIOEngine.swift` gets shorter. Engine validation refuses values
   outside the rule; profile import clamps to it and reports what it clamped.
3. **Nyquist bands** keep being skipped by the engine (they cannot render) but show it: the band row reads
   "Inactive above Nyquist (44.1 kHz)", "N ACTIVE" counts `.active` only, and a page note appears while
   any band is inactive. No band is edited or removed.
4. **Errors are surfaced** through the existing `ProductionActionErrorBanner`; a rollback that fails says
   the profile may be partly applied. No `try?` or empty `catch` remains in the five sites.
5. **EQ page redraw.** The analyser spectrum moves into its own observable model read only by the overlay
   view; one compiled response per (configuration, rate) is cached and shared by the graph, the band
   curves and the headroom badge.
6. **Drag publishing (R6).** Confirm by counting rebuilds and live posts over a scripted 30-step drag. If
   commits rebuild, parameter-only edits (gain, frequency, Q of existing bands) go as live PEQ posts, and
   auto preamp rides the same publish. Topology changes (add, remove, type, phase mode) still rebuild.
7. **Meter cadence.** One rule, `MeterPollPolicy(shouldPoll, lifecycle, page) -> interval or idle`: hidden
   or occluded means idle whatever the lifecycle; stopped and visible draws once and then waits for a
   lifecycle change. Gain-reduction views attach at the rate their view asks (Meters > Dynamics and the
   Dynamics page bars 30 Hz, navigator rows 10 Hz), the monitor loop waits on the hub rather than
   sleeping 16.7 ms, and the telemetry text reads the hub's latest frame (no third poll).
   `TimelineView`s use a paused schedule while the monitor has no new frame and bars are at rest, and
   drop `.id(context.date)`. Per-tick values move to child observable models, so the page body does not
   re-run per tick. Demand is token-based: a page's teardown releases only its own token.
8. **Loudness history** counts appended blocks with a monotonic counter (no freeze after a trim), and
   integrated loudness and LRA come from fixed histograms (EBU Tech 3341/3342 gating; 0.01 dB bins),
   O(bins) per readout, within 0.01 LU of the exact sort.
9. **Peak labels.** "dBTP" only when device rate × detection factor is at least 176.4 kHz (4x at 44.1 kHz,
   the usual BS.1770-4 practice); otherwise "dBFS peak".
10. **Clip latch.** A count lower than the stored baseline is a counter reset: re-baseline, and latch if the
    new count is above zero.
11. **Gain-reduction telemetry (R8)** excludes makeup in the compressor (and multiband if it shares the
    pattern): `grMax = max(grMax, makeup - gainDB)`. Audio is unchanged.
12. **Multiband crossover edits (R10)** run old and new split filters in parallel and crossfade over 5 ms,
    as the PEQ live crossfade does; the second state set is preallocated at build. No allocation.
13. **Limiter attack (R7, flagged)**: the core caps the effective attack at the lookahead,
    `atkHigh = min(atkHigh, max(1, laHigh))`, so zero lookahead means an instant attack. The page shows
    "Attack limited to the lookahead" when the cap applies; stored values are not changed.
14. **Expander timing (R9, flagged)**: Attack is opening speed and Release closing speed (the common gate
    and expander convention). Stored values keep their numbers, so saved profiles open faster.
15. **True-peak factor (R12, flagged)**: the smallest of 1x, 2x, 4x with rate × factor at least 176.4 kHz
    (4x at 44.1/48, 2x at 88.2/96, 1x at 176.4 kHz and above); the reported latency follows it.
16. **Flags.** S1–S4 are flagless: they remove clicks, dips or wasted work, or let an edit the UI already
    offers apply (R1), each proved by its test. S5's three changes alter what plays, so they sit behind
    `e32.dynamics-sound` (off renders exactly as today; only Swift reads it). S6 graduates it. With it on,
    the E23 S1 kernel corpus records them as parity exceptions.
17. **Not planned.** Per-sample dB conversions in compressor, expander and multiband (R12): the owner kept
    per-sample math (E6 G45); the 384 kHz budget is E20 G33's. `N60Protection.c` telemetry is frozen.
18. **Cross-references.** R15 contradicts the closed E28 G3 (loop idles when hidden). E28 G46 (cold
    compressor ring on a lookahead edit) is unchanged by R11. E6 G55 (synchronous `setEQPhaseMode`,
    `ProductionEqualizerView.swift:227`) stays E6's unless the owner moves it (Q5). No existing E6, E27
    or E28 gap or open slice owns R1–R22.
